## OPENING (first ~1800 characters, verbatim)
Chair Bruce Westerman, Ranking Members, and all Members of the Committee — thank you for the opportunity to testify. My name is CW3 (Ret) Brian Wachtendorf. I served honorably for 17 years as a Senior Non-Commissioned Officer and Warrant Officer. I can no longer be silently complicit in the corruption, maltreatment, and retaliatory actions my family and I endured while serving our Nation. I am here to tell the truth about how systemic failures — privatized housing, negligent leaders, and policies implemented that destroyed my career, harmed my family’s health, and jeopardized the Soldiers I led. The bills under consideration — focus on safer, modern infrastructure, empowering agencies to renovate unsafe housing, and improving federal-land agency efficiency — would address the very failures that harmed us. (2017–2020) I was rated among the best in my field and was hand-selected for PCS to the 25th Infantry Division. This PCS triggered a chain of administrative and housing failures. The root cause was not individual error but unclear policy and slow agency action. Had legislation given clear authority and timelines to fix housing and administrative failures, this harm would have been reduced and/or eliminated. Specific housing failures compounded the damage: an informal dispute over 6714 Iwa Lane (New Construction, concluded 13–18 May 2022) ended without mutual agreement; personal belongings were never fully returned, the items that were returned in June 2022 were improperly cleaned and/or missing, causing allergic reactions and numerous ER visits. Reports of mold and a leaky upstairs bathroom began in November 2021, yet residents had to repeatedly request displacement (requested 27 Apr 2022).
