Our company started out as a wireless company in the early 2000s. We have since migrated our focus to all fiber optic networking. We have done this because the wireless and copper technologies have not kept up with the consumer demand. Our customers want fast, reliable Internet access. Whether it is for business or pleasure, that is what our society is demanding. The Internet of Things is growing exponentially. Connected devices are the norm, whether it is a toaster, a car wash, or a vehicle. We have embraced the technology as users but have overlooked the aspect of infrastructure.
