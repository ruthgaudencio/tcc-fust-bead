Testimony by Laura Galante Before the House Committee on Energy and Commerce, Subcommittee on Communications and Technology On “Global Networks at Risk: Securing the Future of Telecommunications Infrastructure” 30 April 2025 Honorable Chair, Ranking Member, and esteemed members of this Committee, thank you for the opportunity to testify on the security of US telecommunications networks and their role in national security and US competitiveness. My name is Laura Galante. I served as the Intelligence Community’s Cyber Executive and the Director of the Cyber Threat Intelligence Integration Center (CTIIC) at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) from January 2022 to January 2025. Prior to this role, I led organizations that track, attribute, and expose cyber operations. I’ll focus my remarks today on national security considerations for the US telecom sector. The Telecommunications Sector in the Cross Hairs This Committee recognizes that reliable and affordable telecom services form the backbone of America’s digital infrastructure, enabling America’s economic engine and global competitiveness. The growth of the telecom sector has closely mirrored the digital transformation of our economy. Over the past 25 years, telecom companies have evolved from phone service providers into complex, multi-service digital organizations, navigating the convergence of communications, media, and technology.
