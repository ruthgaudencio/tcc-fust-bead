Question from the Honorable Randy Weber, “As a streaming provider, does FuboTV negotiate directly with broadcasters to pay retransmission fees for local content?” As a reminder, there are two components of a local linear feed - there are national rights (NFL, college football, etc.) acquired by the Big 4 Networks/broadcasters which are distributed to the appropriate local affiliate i.e. Houston Texans/CBS/KHOU. The second component is the programming produced by the local station groups that typically includes non-primetime programming such as local news and weather, local morning shows and syndicated programming (game shows, etc.). We negotiate with the broadcasters who serve as proxies for the local station groups. This is an extremely efficient process that allows us to blanket nearly every local market in the country.
