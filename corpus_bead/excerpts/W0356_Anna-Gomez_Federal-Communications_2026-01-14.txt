Gomez 2 THE HONORABLE DORIS MATSUI (D-CA): Question 1: During the hearing, you described how the FCC’s news distortion policy does not allow the FCC to revoke or not renew a broadcast license for perceived bias. Is there any historical precedent or legal basis for the FCC to revoke or not renew a broadcast license for perceived bias? Answer: No. The Commission’s authority to revoke broadcast licenses is constrained by the First Amendment and Section 326 of the Communications Act, which specifically prohibits the Commission from censoring broadcasts. (a) Would the Nexstar-Tegna merger make local news more independent? Or more easily pressured into repeating the same message everywhere? Answer: I believe the proposed consolidation would make the merged broadcast stations less independent and more easily pressured into repeating the same message everywhere. The Communications Act and the Commission’s media ownership rules require broadcasters to operate in the public interest.
