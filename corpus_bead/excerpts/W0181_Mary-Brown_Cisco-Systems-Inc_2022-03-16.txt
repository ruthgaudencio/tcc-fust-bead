## ASK SENTENCES (verbatim)
- Most fundamentally, Congress should make clear to NTIA and to the Executive Branch generally, that it wants NTIA to be the lead agency on spectrum matters.
- However, Congress must be mindful that the lab needs to be funded to do whatever it is asked to do, and its evaluations should not be used for delay.
- Parties bring evidence to them, and the FCC’s expert staff work to understand and evaluate that evidence, ultimately providing a recommendation to the Commission on the best resolution for rulemaking or licensing.
- Congress should ask these experts what problems should be solved first, and give them the means to move forward.

## EQUITY/DIVIDE SENTENCES (verbatim)
- More significantly, reverse auctions for rural high-cost support mean that the discipline of the auction reveals the least amount of subsidies the FCC needs to dedicate to achieve its universal services objectives.
