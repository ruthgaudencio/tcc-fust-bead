## EQUITY/DIVIDE SENTENCES (verbatim)
- Today, I will discuss how AT&T is ensuring that FirstNet delivers on Congress’s vision to create a resilient, interoperable broadband network for America’s first responders and the status of FirstNet’s nationwide deployment and adoption.
- FirstNet Deployment, Coverage, and Adoption: Status and Progress The Initial Build 3 In March 2023, AT&T completed the initial build of the network both on-time and on-budget.
- As part of the initial FirstNet build, AT&T collaborated with small and rural wireless providers to quickly expand the reach of FirstNet and launched 2,000 FirstNet tower sites in rural and tribal areas.
- Today, the FirstNet coverage footprint spans nearly 3 million square miles across urban, suburban, rural, tribal, and territorial areas.
- FirstNet in Action FirstNet adoption continues to grow steadily and currently supports nearly 8 million connections, serving nearly 30,600 public safety agencies and direct-support organizations.
- FirstNet’s build-out has leveraged local labor, construction firms, and regional suppliers, driving regional economic activity in rural and urban communities.
- In addition, the network is focused on serving all public safety by ensuring that traditionally underserved communities, like rural and tribal areas, now benefit from the same baseline of reliable communications.
- FirstNet has lowered barriers to entry for small, rural and resource-constrained agencies to have access to advanced broadband tools and reliable connectivity.
