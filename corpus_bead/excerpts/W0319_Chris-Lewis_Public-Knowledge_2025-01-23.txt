Why America Leads the World in Wireless Tech, and Has for the Last 30 Years. The major wireless innovations of the last 30 years – mobile telephony, Wi-Fi, advanced navigation systems – all began in the United States. Not only were they invented here, but they also quickly spread and created entire new sections of our economy. This is astounding, when one thinks about it. How has the United States maintained such dominance? Three factors give the United States its unprecedented staying power as the leader in wireless.
