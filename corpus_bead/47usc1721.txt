# 47 U.S.C. § 1721 — Definitions
# source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/1721
# obtido em: 2026-10-27
# nota: esta seção é de definições do subcapítulo (não a seção operativa
# do programa BEAD propriamente); citada como tal pelo pedido original.

In this subchapter:

(1) Adoption of broadband means the process by which an individual obtains daily access to the internet at a speed, quality, and capacity necessary for common tasks that qualifies as an advanced telecommunications capability, with requisite digital skills, on a personal device and secure network.

(2) Advanced telecommunications capability has the meaning given in section 1302(d).

(3) Aging individual has the meaning given "older individual" in section 3002 of title 42.

(4) Appropriate committees of Congress means specified Senate and House committees on Appropriations, Commerce/Transportation, and Energy/Commerce.

(5) Assistant Secretary means the Assistant Secretary of Commerce for Communications and Information.

(6) Community anchor institution includes public schools, housing authorities, libraries, healthcare providers, community colleges, State library agencies, and nonprofit/governmental support organizations.

(7) Covered household means a household with income not exceeding 150% of poverty level per Census Bureau criteria.

(8) Covered populations includes individuals in covered households, aging persons, incarcerated individuals (excluding federal), veterans, persons with disabilities, those with language barriers, racial/ethnic minorities, and rural residents.

(9) Covered programs means the State Digital Equity Capacity Grant Program and Digital Equity Competitive Grant Program.

(10) Digital equity means the condition enabling individuals and communities to have information technology capacity for full societal and economic participation.

(11) Digital inclusion encompasses activities ensuring access to affordable information/communication technologies, digital literacy training, technical support, and cybersecurity awareness.

(12) Digital literacy means skills for finding, evaluating, organizing, creating, and communicating information using technology.

(13) Disability has the meaning in section 12102 of title 42.

(14) Eligible State means a State with Assistant Secretary-approved applications under specified sections.

(15) Gender identity has the meaning in section 249(c) of title 18.

(16) Indian Tribe has the meaning in section 5304(e) of title 25.

(17) Institution of higher education has the meaning in section 1001 of title 20, including postsecondary vocational institutions.

(18) Local educational agency has the meaning in section 7801(30) of title 20.

(19) Postsecondary vocational institution has the meaning in section 1002(c) of title 20.

(20) Rural area has the meaning in section 950bb(b)(3) of title 7.

(21) State means any State, the District of Columbia, or Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.

(22) Veteran has the meaning in section 101 of title 38.

(23) Workforce development program has the meaning in section 3102(66) of title 29.
